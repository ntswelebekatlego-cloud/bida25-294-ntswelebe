CHROME HEARTS HISTORY

The jewellery company Chrome Hearts was founded in the late 1980s with a focus on intricately designed silver accessories. It added apparel, eyeglasses and leather goods to its lineup as time went on. Thanks to its rebellious design and meticulous attention to detail, the brand soon developed a cult following among celebrities and fashion fans. Rooted in rock 'n' roll ethos and sterling silver craftsmanship, Chrome Hearts expanded from a small Hollywood workshop into a global gothic luxury empire.


ABOUT US

Chrome Hearts is more than a brand — it's a movement. We are craftsmen, rebels, and storytellers. From our Los Angeles atelier to boutiques worldwide, we create objects that transcend fashion: hand-carved silver, hand-painted leather, and limited-run apparel that defies convention.
