THE LEGACY

Since 1988, Chrome Hearts has remained an icon of gothic luxury. Founded by Richard Stark, the brand merges sterling silver craftsmanship with rock & roll attitude. Each piece is handcrafted in Los Angeles and ateliers worldwide, embodying rebellion, artistry, and timeless elegance. Our Botswana flagship brings this dark romance to Africa.
